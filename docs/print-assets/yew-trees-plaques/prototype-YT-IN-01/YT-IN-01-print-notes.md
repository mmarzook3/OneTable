# YT-IN-01 prototype print notes

This prototype is for **The Yew Trees - Indoor Table 1**.

- Permanent Scanaki URL: `https://scanaki.uk/p/lzq3ovWCgJ1IYUnD`
- Finished size: **80 x 110 x 2.1 mm**
- Base thickness: **2.0 mm**
- Raised QR and text: **0.1 mm**
- Screw holes: **2 x 4 mm through holes**
- Countersinks: **8 mm diameter x 1 mm deep**
- Rear NFC recess: **27 mm diameter x 0.8 mm deep**, suitable for a nominal 25 mm NFC tag

## Recommended prototype print

1. Import the STL at 100% scale and keep millimetres as the unit.
2. Print the plaque flat with the text and QR facing upward.
3. Use a **0.10 mm layer height**. The requested 0.1 mm relief is one layer high and may disappear with a 0.2 mm layer profile.
4. Use a 0.4 mm or smaller nozzle, three or four walls, and 15-20% infill.
5. For a contrasting QR and text, pause or change filament at **2.0 mm**. Alternatively, paint only the raised surface after printing.
6. Use countersunk M3.5/M4 wood screws that fit the 4 mm through holes and 8 mm countersinks. Confirm the screw head fit before mounting.
7. Program a 25 mm NFC tag with the same permanent URL, test it, then fix it into the rear recess. Do not permanently lock the tag until the full customer flow has been tested.
8. Scan the physical QR from several angles and distances before approving the remaining plaques.

## Digital validation completed

- The embossed QR raster decoded to the exact permanent URL.
- The STL dimensions are 80 x 110 x 2.1 mm.
- The mesh is watertight, consistently wound, and one connected solid.

Digital validation does not replace printing and testing this first physical prototype.
